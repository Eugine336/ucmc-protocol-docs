# Security Policy

## Supported Versions

This repository contains protocol documentation, not implementation code.

## How to Report a Vulnerability

Please report security concerns to: **security@ucmccore.tech**

Include:

- A clear description of the issue
- Affected document sections
- Reproduction or reasoning steps
- Potential impact

Please avoid public disclosure until the issue is assessed and coordinated.

## Scope

In scope for this repository:

- Vulnerabilities in documented protocol design
- Cryptographic construction concerns
- Threat-model weaknesses in the published documentation

## Out of Scope

Out of scope for this repository:

- Deployments of UCMC by third parties
- The live `ucmccore.tech` site (separate disclosure channel)
- Non-security editorial issues (open a standard issue for those)

## Response SLA

- Acknowledgement within **5 business days**
- Triage and resolution timeline depends on severity and required coordination

## Safe Harbor

We support good-faith security research. If you act in good faith, avoid privacy violations, avoid service disruption, and do not access or modify data beyond what is necessary to demonstrate the issue, we will not pursue legal action for your research activities.
